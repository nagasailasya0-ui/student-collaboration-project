Who Was Ravana?

Ravana was the mighty king of Lanka in the Hindu epic Ramayana. He was known for his intelligence, strength, musical talent, and deep knowledge of ancient scriptures. He was also a great devotee of Lord Shiva.


The Abduction of Sita

According to the Ramayana, Ravana abducted Sita, the wife of Lord Rama, and took her to Lanka. Lord Rama, together with Lakshmana and an army of Vanaras led by Hanuman and Sugriva, travelled to Lanka to rescue her.

The Great Battle

A great battle took place between Rama's army and Ravana's forces. After a fierce struggle, Rama defeated Ravana and rescued Sita.


The Lesson of Ravana's Story

Ravana's story reminds us that knowledge and power are valuable, but pride and wrongful actions can lead to destruction. In the Ramayana tradition, Rama's victory represents the triumph of dharma over adharma.
